Sierra Leone is a beautiful country in West Africa, known for its beautiful beaches, green mountains, rich culture, and friendly people. Its capital city is Freetown, which is located along the Atlantic Ocean. Sierra Leone is rich in natural resources and has a history of resilience, unity, and cultural diversity.
